1. classify_request
2. resolve_employee
3. query_pto_database
4. retrieve_documents
5. generate_response
6. handle_error

    resolve_employee -> query_pto_database -> generate_response -> END

    handle_error -> END

PTO intent includes questions containing concepts such as:

- PTO balance
- current PTO
- vacation balance
- sick balance
- sick hours

- available leave
